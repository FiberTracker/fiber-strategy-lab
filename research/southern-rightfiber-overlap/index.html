Where Southern targets add reach to Rightfiber

The measured Yelcot and Aeneas components overlap the historical Ritter footprint at only 1.04% and 1.17% of their reported locations. Louisiana candidates require a geographic expansion case: neither Pelican nor Cajun shares a county with the measured Ritter residential-capable FTTP footprint.

This is a December 31, 2025 location-availability comparison of selected historical legal filers. It does not certify the current combined Rightfiber network, construction, passings, customers or unserved market opportunity.

Arkansas: prioritize transport diligence around Yelcot; keep NATCO separate

Yelcot reports 12,274 eligible locations, with 128 shared with Ritter, all in Independence County. Its 12,146 other locations form a candidate expansion footprint, subject to current network and ownership verification. NATCO has 5,573 locations with no exact overlap; Ritter reports only five eligible locations in their shared county, Boone. NATCO and Yelcot themselves share 53 locations in Marion County, so their footprints cannot simply be added. The next useful evidence is route maps, interconnection points and operating economics.

Louisiana: expansion logic must replace an assumed local infill story

Pelican reports 8,614 and Cajun 5,465 eligible locations, with zero same-location overlap. Neither has a county in common with the measured Ritter component, which has 4,687 eligible locations in Lincoln Parish. This does not contradict business transport or later builds: business-only records and 2026 construction are outside this test. Underwrite physical connections, maintenance coverage and new-market execution before attributing operating synergies.

Aeneas: low duplication does not settle who can sell which assets

Aeneas reports 77,976 eligible Tennessee locations; 910 overlap Ritter, including 886 in Tipton County, 15 in Fayette and nine in Shelby. The 77,066 locations outside Ritter are a reporting-set difference. Southwest Tennessee Electric Membership Corporation board records establish Aeneas acquisition and control context. Any transaction thesis must establish the operating company perimeter, cooperative-owned network rights and consent requirements.
