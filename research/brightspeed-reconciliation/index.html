Brightspeed: reconcile FCC availability with the build story

The full 20-state filing count is 1.79 million residential-capable FTTP locations. Brightspeed separately reported more than 3 million fiber-enabled locations in April 2026. Geography explains part of the original shortfall; the remaining definitions still need an address-level bridge.

The public build story is larger than the FCC availability count

Brightspeed reported more than 3 million fiber-enabled locations on April 2, 2026. A fresh count of every current D25 provider file for Connect Holding II LLC, provider 470022, produces 1,793,485 unique technology-50 residential-capable locations across 20 states. These are different dated measures; neither should replace the other without a location-and-unit bridge. [P] P01 [P] FCCCAT

The FCC count now covers every state listed for this verified filer. That resolves the prior geographical omission, but does not prove that no additional legal filer should be included. No additional filer increment has been established by the reviewed corporate records. Legacy CenturyLink or United Telephone names alone are insufficient. [P] P09 [P] P12

Use the right corporate perimeter

The 20-state Lumen ILEC transaction closed October 3, 2022. FCC approval was adopted and released August 19; Lumen announced approval August 22. Approval and closing are separate dates. [P] P09 [P] P10 [P] P11

The December 2023 FCC ruling places Connect Holding II beneath Connect Holding, then Connect Midco, Connect Intermediate and Connect Parent. It calls Connect Holding II immediate parent of ten wireless licensees; that does not make it direct parent of every operating company. [P] P12

Lumen retained national fiber routes and associated networks, along with ILEC operations in 16 states. Do not move retained Lumen, Qwest or Level 3 assets into Brightspeed by name similarity. [P] P11

South Carolina approved selected affiliate fiber-asset transfers and leasebacks on August 14, 2026. The order does not prove consummation, exact-span title or implementation in all 20 states. The April 17 application provides Fiber Connection and Fiber Services name histories, including June 2, 2025 changes. These entity changes establish no extra FCC locations by themselves. [P] P13 [P] P14

The next useful data request

  • One dated address/BSL/unit bridge: acquired fiber, newly constructed passing, ready for service, orderable location, installed account and paying subscriber.
  • Monthly build and service-ready cohorts by state and legal filer, with the same units and a reconciliation to the filed FCC submission.
  • A current legal-entity and FCC-provider crosswalk, including transferred affiliates and retained Lumen assets, with duplicate-location controls.
  • Keep the public thresholds, measured FCC locations and any management figures in separate labeled columns until this bridge is delivered.
