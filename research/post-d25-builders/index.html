Post-D25 builders: LiveOak Fiber, Wire 3 and IQ Fiber

As of September 20, 2026 | Public sources only

These three profiles correct a timing problem in the December 31, 2025 FCC D25 snapshot. LiveOak started a South Carolina build in September 2026, Wire 3 launched Lake County service and announced Martin County construction in 2026, and IQ Fiber launched Pinellas County service in May 2026. Their newer activity cannot appear in a 2025 year-end dataset. The absence of a state or county row therefore does not establish that the operator lacks a network there today. Planned builds, construction underway and live service remain separate evidence stages. [D: D01; P: S01]

The FCC figures below are residential-capable R/X FTTP availability locations reported by each legal filer. They are useful as a dated availability perimeter. They are not company passings, subscribers, route miles or 2026 construction progress. RightFiber core proximity was not measured here. Each company therefore carries a regional-distance penalty, especially in Florida, rather than an assumed adjacency benefit.

LiveOak Fiber

LiveOak's current site identifies operating communities in Bay, Okaloosa, Santa Rosa and Walton counties in Florida and Chatham and Glynn counties in Georgia. It also says the company is “Proudly Supported By” InfraRed Capital Partners. The public evidence supports describing LiveOak as an InfraRed-backed platform, but it does not disclose InfraRed's exact ownership percentage. [P: LiveOak About, accessed Sep. 20, 2026, S02]

The post-D25 correction is South Carolina. On September 10, LiveOak said construction had started in Bluffton as a northward extension of its Savannah network. The project targets more than 10,000 homes and businesses, with first customers expected online in early 2027. Hardeeville and neighboring communities were future phases. This is construction evidence, not current service, and the lack of an SC filer-state entry in D25 is fully consistent with a 2026 start. [P: Bluffton construction, Sep. 10, 2026, S04]

LiveOak reported a $425 million credit facility from Oak Hill Advisors and Palistar Capital on April 9, 2026. InfraRed described the platform it had built alongside LiveOak. This supports independent growth capacity and sponsor continuity. It does not make OHA or Palistar equity owners, and $425 million is facility capacity rather than drawn debt or deployed capital. [P: LiveOak financing, Apr. 9, 2026, S03]

The strategic fit is a coherent coastal Southeast cluster with a local operating model and a Savannah-to-Bluffton extension. Potential routes include an owner-level combination, wholesale or construction partnership, or financing dialogue. The public record does not identify a seller or active process. The countercase is strong: LiveOak has a fresh credit facility, active sponsor support and current expansion. No current public passings, subscribers or EBITDA were located, and operating proximity to RightFiber remains unmeasured.

Wire 3

Oak Hill Capital acquired Wire 3 from Guggenheim Investments in January 2024 and committed up to $250 million. Management retained shares. A July 2025 release continued to call Oak Hill the majority investor and separately reported a $235 million private credit financing arranged by Guggenheim. The ownership conclusion is clear, but the capital figures should not be added or presented as spend. [P: Oak Hill transaction, Jan. 4, 2024, S05; credit financing, Jul. 10, 2025, S06]

Wire 3's D25 filing shows 119,263 Florida R/X FTTP availability locations, with no Lake or Martin County rows. Newer primary evidence resolves the apparent gap. In April 2026, Wire 3 reported that the first Leesburg homes and businesses were connected, while construction continued neighborhood by neighborhood. That proves some live Lake County service, not countywide completion. The same release described more than $150 million of Lake County investment, which remains an announced investment figure rather than verified spend. [P: Leesburg launch, Apr. 2026, S07]

Martin County was earlier in the cycle. On June 3, Wire 3 announced a $53 million plan for Ocean Breeze, Sewall's Point and Stuart. It forecast summer construction and first service as early as fall. The release alternates between “53,000+ homes and businesses” and “53,000+ residents and businesses,” so the unit must remain qualified. No cited source here upgrades Martin to live service. [P: Martin County plan, Jun. 3, 2026, S08]

Wire 3 also reported construction underway in Albany, Georgia on June 23 and Anderson, South Carolina on August 4, with eventual targets above 37,000 and nearly 35,000 homes and businesses, respectively. Those targets are not passings. [P: Albany construction, S09; Anderson construction, S10]

Because Wire 3 is already Oak Hill-led, an acquisition framing is misplaced. The practical analytical routes are sponsor-level portfolio coordination, operating combination, or wholesale and build cooperation. The countercase is its separately funded expansion plan, geographic distance from an unmeasured RightFiber core and the execution load of simultaneous three-state construction. No sale process is inferred.

IQ Fiber

IQ Fiber's latest public ownership trail identifies SDC Capital Partners as its founding investor. SDC provided a $150 million follow-on equity commitment in 2023 and additional equity backing for the announced ThinkBig Networks acquisition in 2024. The current site lists Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Maryland and South Carolina locations. These sources support “SDC-backed,” but do not disclose SDC's current ownership percentage. [P: SDC commitment, S11; ThinkBig announcement, S12; current locations, S14]

D25 records 127,178 Florida R/X FTTP availability locations and 578 in Charleston County, South Carolina for IQ Fiber, but no Pinellas County row. On May 1, 2026, IQ Fiber launched service in parts of St. Petersburg, Clearwater and Largo. It said the launch began a regional investment of more than $100 million and that deployment would continue for another 18 to 24 months. The proper status is live initial service plus ongoing construction, not completed countywide coverage. The announced investment is not spend to date. [P: Pinellas launch, May 1, 2026, S13]

IQ Fiber shows both organic construction and acquisition-led expansion. That makes it relevant for strategic monitoring and a possible sponsor-to-sponsor, operating or wholesale discussion. Public evidence does not establish transaction availability. The countercase is continued SDC growth funding, a five-state footprint that may add integration complexity, and no measured RightFiber adjacency. No current public passings, subscribers or EBITDA were located.
