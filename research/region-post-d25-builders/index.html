LiveOak Fiber

Scaled strategic platform | P2: strategic monitoring

Current ownership

InfraRed Capital Partners-supported platform. LiveOak's current About page says it is proudly supported by InfraRed, and an April 2026 financing release describes the platform built alongside InfraRed. The public sources reviewed do not disclose InfraRed's exact ownership percentage. Oak Hill Advisors and Palistar provided a $425 million credit facility and are not classified here as equity owners.

Commercial fit (inference)

The Savannah-to-Bluffton extension shows a contiguous Lowcountry build pattern and local operating model that could be relevant to a Southeast cluster strategy. Fit with RightFiber remains an unmeasured geographic and operating hypothesis; Florida and coastal Georgia/SC are outside a demonstrated core adjacency in this work.

Possible transaction or partnership

Analytical routes are an owner-level combination, construction/wholesale partnership or financing dialogue. Public evidence supports none as an active process and does not identify LiveOak as a seller.

Constraints

Bluffton was under construction, not in service, on 2026-09-10.; No current public passings, subscribers or EBITDA were located.; Fresh sponsor support and a new credit facility indicate independent growth capacity.; The D25 absence of South Carolina is a vintage limitation, not evidence that LiveOak lacks a current SC build.

Countercase

The new $425 million facility, active sponsor and multi-state expansion could favor continued standalone growth. Geographic separation and an unmeasured RightFiber-core distance may limit near-term operating synergies.

Geographic clusters

  • geography: Florida Panhandle; markets: Bay County; Okaloosa County; Santa Rosa County; Walton County; stage as of: service-area page/current operations
  • geography: Coastal Georgia; markets: Chatham County; Glynn County; stage as of: service-area page/current operations
  • geography: South Carolina Lowcountry; markets: Bluffton; stage as of: construction started 2026-09-10; first customers expected early 2027
  • geography: South Carolina Lowcountry; markets: Hardeeville; neighboring communities; stage as of: future phases planned as of 2026-09-10

Construction evidence

  • date: 2026-09-10; stage: construction; claim: LiveOak announced construction had started in Bluffton, extending north from Savannah; the project targets more than 10,000 homes and businesses, with first customers expected in early 2027.
  • date: 2026-09-20 access; stage: service-area evidence; claim: The current company site lists operating communities in Bay, Okaloosa, Santa Rosa and Walton counties in Florida and Chatham and Glynn counties in Georgia.

Reported scale and date

  • 83,741 FCC D25 residential-capable R/X FTTP availability locations (2025-12-31; Florida only, filer 480055; availability locations, not passings or subscribers)
  • more than 10,000 homes and businesses targeted (2026-09-10; Bluffton, SC; construction-stage target; no service yet confirmed)
  • 425,000,000 USD credit facility (2026-04-09; company financing; facility size, not drawn debt or spend)

Wire 3

Oak Hill-backed portfolio platform | P2: portfolio coordination

Current ownership

Oak Hill Capital majority-owned; management retained shares. Oak Hill acquired Wire 3 from Guggenheim Investments in January 2024 and committed up to $250 million; a July 2025 release continued to identify Oak Hill as majority investor. Guggenheim arranged a $235 million private credit financing in 2025. Credit and total capital raised are not treated as ownership or cash spent.

Commercial fit (inference)

Wire 3 has a repeatable retail FTTH expansion model across Florida and adjacent Southeast states. The Lake and Martin examples demonstrate how new builds can be invisible in D25. Any RightFiber fit remains analytical, with a Florida regional-distance penalty and no measured adjacency.

Possible transaction or partnership

Because Oak Hill already owns a majority stake, the clean analytical route is sponsor-level portfolio coordination, operating combination or wholesale/construction partnership. No public sale process is inferred.

Constraints

Lake service began after the D25 vintage; Martin remained a plan at the cited date.; The public source uses inconsistent units for Martin's 53,000-plus target.; Raised-capital figures combine equity commitments and credit and should not be treated as deployed capital.; No current public passings, subscribers or EBITDA were located.

Countercase

Wire 3 is already Oak Hill-backed and funded for a broad independent expansion. The Florida-led footprint is geographically distant from an unmeasured RightFiber core, while three-state construction adds execution demands.

Geographic clusters

  • geography: Lake County, FL; markets: Leesburg; stage as of: first homes and businesses connected in April 2026; continuing build
  • geography: Martin County, FL; markets: Ocean Breeze; Sewall's Point; Stuart; stage as of: announced plan on 2026-06-03; summer construction and fall service were expectations
  • geography: Georgia; markets: Albany; Macon; stage as of: construction underway in Albany by 2026-06-23; service timing remained an expectation
  • geography: South Carolina; markets: Anderson; stage as of: construction underway by 2026-08-04; first service expected fall 2026

Construction evidence

  • date: 2026-04; stage: service launch; claim: Wire 3 reported the first Leesburg homes and businesses connected; construction continued neighborhood by neighborhood.
  • date: 2026-06-03; stage: planned; claim: Wire 3 announced a $53 million Martin County plan targeting more than 53,000 residents/homes and businesses; construction and first-service dates were forecasts.
  • date: 2026-06-23; stage: construction; claim: Wire 3 reported Albany construction underway, with more than 37,000 homes and businesses as the eventual access target.
  • date: 2026-08-04; stage: construction; claim: Wire 3 reported construction underway in Anderson, its first South Carolina market, with nearly 35,000 homes and businesses as the eventual target.

IQ Fiber

Scaled strategic platform | P2: strategic monitoring

Current ownership

SDC Capital Partners-backed platform. IQ Fiber identified SDC as its founding investor in a 2023 follow-on equity announcement and said the planned ThinkBig acquisition was backed by additional SDC equity in 2024. The public sources reviewed do not disclose SDC's current ownership percentage. The latest site confirms the expanded five-state operating footprint but not a control percentage.

Commercial fit (inference)

IQ Fiber demonstrates both organic builds and acquisition-led expansion across multiple Atlantic states. Pinellas is a strong example of live 2026 service absent from a 2025-12-31 snapshot. A combined-platform fit is plausible only at a high level; Florida carries a regional-distance penalty and RightFiber core proximity was not measured.

Possible transaction or partnership

Analytical routes are sponsor-to-sponsor combination, regional operating partnership or wholesale collaboration. Public evidence does not identify IQ Fiber or SDC as a seller.

Constraints

The Pinellas launch proves some service, not countywide completion.; The more-than-$100 million figure is an announced regional investment, not spend to date.; The 2024 ThinkBig release described a signed transaction subject to approvals; current Maryland service listings support an expanded footprint but this module does not reconstruct closing mechanics.; No current public passings, subscribers or EBITDA were located.

Countercase

SDC has repeatedly supplied growth equity, and IQ Fiber has expanded organically and through acquisition. Its five-state operating span may introduce integration complexity without demonstrated RightFiber adjacency or shared systems.

Geographic clusters

  • geography: Florida; markets: Jacksonville; Gainesville; Pinellas County; stage as of: live service, with continuing Pinellas deployment
  • geography: Georgia and South Carolina coast; markets: Savannah; Charleston; stage as of: current service-area listing
  • geography: Delaware and Maryland Chesapeake Bay; markets: Dover; Calvert; Cecil; Charles; Harford; Kent; Queen Anne's; stage as of: current service-area listing

Construction evidence

  • date: 2026-05-01; stage: service launch plus construction; claim: IQ Fiber launched service in parts of St. Petersburg, Clearwater and Largo, while saying network deployment would continue for another 18 to 24 months.
  • date: 2026-09-20 access; stage: current service-area evidence; claim: The company site lists Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Maryland and South Carolina locations.

Reported scale and date

  • 127,178 FCC D25 residential-capable R/X FTTP availability locations (2025-12-31; Florida only, filer 470041; availability locations, not passings or subscribers; no Pinellas row)
  • 578 FCC D25 residential-capable R/X FTTP availability locations (2025-12-31; Charleston County, SC, filer 470041; availability locations, not total South Carolina footprint)
  • more than $100 million USD regional investment announced (2026-05-01; Pinellas County, FL; launch marked the beginning of investment; not amount spent)
  • 18-24 months of expected continuing deployment (2026-05-01; Pinellas County, FL; forward-looking schedule)
