NATCO Communications, Inc.

Independent operator screen | P1: investigate

Current ownership

Independent, locally operated; NATCO says third-generation Sanders family remains involved. No public sale announcement found in this bounded pass.

Commercial fit (inference)

Best immediate partner. It fills a north-central gap where Rightfiber reported only five Boone locations, and the BEAD partnership is a demonstrated channel. Local ownership and funded builds favor partnership before M&A.

Possible transaction or partnership

Explore on transport, construction, purchasing, managed services, and grant execution. Keep minority investment or acquisition as a family-initiated option only.

Constraints

Ozark density/terrain; USDA/BEAD obligations; ILEC/ETC approvals; family governance; small-system economics.

Countercase

NATCO may gain sufficient scale through cooperation without selling, while Rightfiber is still integrating its September merger.

Geographic clusters

  • Boone 2,132 and Marion 3,441 D25 FTTP availability locations; NATCO also markets parts of Baxter, but its D25 filer had no Baxter FTTP records.

Construction evidence

  • USDA approved a $3.287 million FY2024 grant plus $0.493 million additional funding for Marion County FTTP benefiting 242 residents and two businesses.
  • NATCO reports $12.7 million of BEAD projects underway/planned for 1,500+ locations. Arkansas lists an E. Ritter/NATCO BEAD pre-registration partnership; scope and award status were not located.

Reported scale and date

  • 5,573 unique FCC FTTP availability locations, not passings or subscribers (D25 2025-12-31; revised 2026-09-15)

Yelcot Holding Group, Inc. / Mountain View Telephone Company

Independent operator screen | P1: investigate

Current ownership

Yelcot Telephone, Mountain View Telephone, and Yelcot Video remain under Yelcot Holding Group; the site says locally operated. Beneficial ownership was not verified and no transaction announcement was found.

Commercial fit (inference)

Best scaled footprint. It extends Rightfiber through the Ozarks and overlaps its Independence presence. Marion overlap with NATCO requires address-level analysis.

Possible transaction or partnership

Start with interconnection, backhaul, enterprise referrals, field support, or grant construction; consider holdco acquisition after ownership and subsidy diligence.

Constraints

$25.281 million USDA loan; E-ACAM milestones; multiple regulated entities; legacy cable/voice/IT products; change-of-control review.

Countercase

Committed construction and local control may limit sale appetite; rural integration could distract from Rightfiber's current merger.

Geographic clusters

  • Baxter 3,264; Stone 2,802; Marion 2,280; Independence 2,076; Izard 1,467; smaller Cleburne, Sharp, Searcy, and White records.

Construction evidence

  • USDA awarded Mountain View Telephone a $25.281 million FY2024 FTTP loan benefiting 2,630 people, 46 businesses, 124 farms, and six educational facilities in Izard and Stone. FCC shows 7,323 tentative E-ACAM required locations through 2028.

Reported scale and date

  • 12,274 unique FCC FTTP availability locations, not passings or subscribers (D25 2025-12-31; revised 2026-09-15)

South Arkansas Telephone Company, including ARIS relationship

Independent operator screen | P1: investigate

Current ownership

FCC identifies SATCO as the study-area holding company. SATCO and Ouachita Electric Cooperative formed ARIS; current beneficial ownership and ARIS economics were not verified. No sale announcement was found.

Commercial fit (inference)

Useful southern infill between Rightfiber's Clark/Union activity and SATCO/ARIS markets. Partnership is clearer than acquisition until SATCO versus ARIS rights are separated.

Possible transaction or partnership

Three-party SATCO/OECC discussion on transport, wholesale, enterprise handoffs, operations, and builds; consider M&A only after asset, pole, contract, and governance mapping.

Constraints

ARIS/co-op governance; pole rights; E-ACAM duties; SATCO's large business/unclassified FCC record set; ILEC approvals.

Countercase

Joint governance may block control synergies; Rightfiber's El Dorado build could make Union overlap competitive.

Geographic clusters

  • SATCO filer: Bradley 2,383; Calhoun 2,106; Ouachita 434; Union 227. Separate OECC/SATCO ARIS filer: Ouachita 6,304; Dallas 744; Calhoun 330.

Construction evidence

  • OECC says ARIS targeted all 9,500 cooperative homes/businesses and cites a 300-mile SATCO/ARE-ON network. Arkansas confirms the operating partnership; FCC shows 731 tentative SATCO E-ACAM required locations through 2028.

Reported scale and date

  • 5,150 SATCO FCC FTTP availability locations; separate ARIS filer reports 7,378; neither is passings/subscribers and counts may overlap (D25 2025-12-31; revised 2026-09-15)

PhireLink, including Arkwest

Scaled strategic platform | P2: selective

Current ownership

Management-controlled PhireLink platform. Arkwest is an acquired operating brand, not an independent target. Ultimate controllers identified in FCC filings include Glen Post III, James Davidson and Benjamin Friedman.

Commercial fit (inference)

Arkwest adds a central and western Arkansas operating base near Rightfiber counties. Physical interconnection and incremental locations remain unmeasured. A platform-level partnership is more plausible than treating the recently acquired brand as a free-standing seller.

Possible transaction or partnership

Owner-to-owner interconnection, transport, wholesale, referral, or joint-build discussion. Treat M&A only as a future PhireLink-level combination.

Constraints

Recent control change; PhireLink growth plan; E-ACAM/ETC duties; duplicate integration; ILEC approvals; likely premium.

Countercase

A near-term resale conflicts with PhireLink's stated Arkansas expansion strategy; both owners may target the same River Valley corridor.

Geographic clusters

  • Yell 5,210; Perry 340; Scott 131 D25 FTTP availability locations.
  • St. Tammany and Slidell, Louisiana
  • Yell, Scott, and Perry Counties, Arkansas via Arkwest
  • southeastern Colorado
  • western Kansas

Construction evidence

  • Deal materials describe a 100% FTTP, third-generation GPON network exceeding 900 route miles, built from internal cash flow; current pages say the entire network is fiber. No post-close Arkansas build count was located.
  • FCC records identify Camellia City Fiber and Tammany Wireless as Louisiana affiliates and granted the Arkwest transfer on 2025-07-03. PhireLink announced Arkwest's more than 900-route-mile, 100% FTTP network on 2025-04-29. FCC D25 lists Camellia City Fiber provider 450023 with technology 50.

Reported scale and date

  • 5,681 unique FCC FTTP availability locations, not passings or subscribers (D25 2025-12-31; revised 2026-09-15)
  • More than 900 route miles applies only to the announced Arkwest Arkansas perimeter as of 2025-04-29. Louisiana route miles, passings, and subscribers remain undisclosed. PhireLink's 2025-01-06 BEAD announcement disclosed no dollar amount or location count.
