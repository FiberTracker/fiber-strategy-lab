Midwest ownership, build stages and transaction watch

Prioritize owner-verified commercial routes with Joink, Metro, Midwest Fiber Networks, WIN Technology and selected Iowa, Missouri and Kentucky operators. Keep recent consolidators, cooperatives and public-company platforms in separate lanes, and do not count announced acquisitions as closed.

Public-source review as of September 20, 2026. The operator screen covers Illinois and Indiana plus selected Wisconsin, Iowa, Missouri, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio and Kansas platform cases. County names identify public footprint or project clusters, not route connections, physical diversity, enterprise availability or seller willingness. Scale is stated only with its source date and unit. No new FCC availability counts are calculated or presented. Iowa and Kentucky contain selected named cases from a targeted ten-case review, not exhaustive full-state shortlists. Commercial priorities and countercases are analytical judgments.

Evidence required before advancing a transaction

  • Obtain current cap tables, legal-entity charts and beneficial owners. A brand, sponsor relationship or FCC filer name does not define the sale perimeter.
  • Reconcile closed, announced, approved and operationally transitioning transactions. Citizens, Wave and Ripple remain outside confirmed closed rollups in this review.
  • Require exact A/Z buildings, route GIS, shared-risk groups, owned versus leased plant, IRUs, easements, grant restrictions, liens and assignment rights. County overlap is not a connection.
  • Bridge every scale figure by date and unit: passings, serviceable premises, customers, subscribers, RGUs, route miles, awarded projects and completed construction are different measures.
  • Wisconsin public research identifies credible operators and projects, but the recovered full state FCC source is validated in the separate Wisconsin state model. This model makes no new Wisconsin FCC count.
  • Iowa and Kentucky rows are the strongest selected cases from a targeted ten-case review, not exhaustive full-state shortlists. Additional cooperative and strategic-counterparty cases remain outside this compact screen.
  • Only public sources are used. Surf is included only on its public ownership, financing and operating disclosures.
